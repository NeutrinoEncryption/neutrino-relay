### 2. Summary of Changes
UDP registration is NRU1 + opaque ticket[16] + role[1] (0 or 1). Each peer gets
its own registration echoed on pairing; subsequent payloads forward unchanged.
Same-role registration replaces a changed NAT address. Unknown ciphertext drops.
Pending/total allocations, IP accounting, packet/byte rates, egress, idle time and
absolute lifetime are bounded. No new dependency or version change was needed.

### 3. Detailed Technical Rationale ("The Why")
* **Why selected:** source-address lookup permits prefix-free QUIC forwarding.
  Explicit roles disambiguate NAT rebinding from a new partner. A single socket
  owner avoids shared locks and races across paired state and address indexes.
* **Alternatives rejected:** per-datagram ticket headers impose overhead and leak
  ticket repetition; WebSocket tunnels impose TCP head-of-line blocking. Full
  TURN authentication is outside the requested minimal protocol. Source spoofing
  and ticket holders reclaiming a role are documented limitations; peers still
  require end-to-end authentication.
* **Performance & Memory:** one reusable 65,535-byte heap receive buffer; borrowed
  payload slices, fixed-size notice arrays, no packet queues/copies or payload
  parsing. Allocation tables cap at 4096, pending defaults to 2048, IP rate table
  caps at 16384. One-second cleanup scans bounded tables. Fixed-window limits
  permit boundary bursts; drops rely on QUIC congestion control/retransmission.

### 4. Invariant Compliance Checklist
